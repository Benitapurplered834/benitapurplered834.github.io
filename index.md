## 📊 How Does It Work?  

pdf-inspector works in a clever yet straightforward way:  

1. **It reads your PDF** – Just like opening a book, it looks at every page.  
2. **It looks for hidden text** – Text-based PDFs have invisible text layers that computers can read. Scanned PDFs are just pictures.  
3. **It checks the pixels** – For scanned documents, it examines the images to determine if they look like text pages.  
4. **It reports back** – You get a clear answer: "Text PDF" or "Scanned PDF," plus the extracted content.  

This all happens automatically in less than a second for most files.
